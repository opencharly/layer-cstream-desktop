## Why this repin is skew-free where the previous attempt was not

Until `pod-cstream#15`, **two candies pinned `pod-hyprland`** — this layer and `pod-cstream` itself.
Two pins on one candy report `resolved to multiple versions` every time either side moves, and no
ordering of cross-repo PRs can avoid it because they cannot change atomically. An earlier revision
of this chain measured **37 warnings from 9 skew occurrences** on exactly this bed.

`pod-cstream#15` removed its pin, on the grounds this layer's own description already states — a
metalayer's members *are what it is*, and installing the same thing in two places gives "a consumer
two places to look for the same behaviour". With **one** pin there is nothing left to disagree with,
which is why the gate below is clean rather than argued about.
