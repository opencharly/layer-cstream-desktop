## Why the skew existed, and why both pins move together

`pod-cstream` advanced to `pod-hyprland:v2026.242.2008` while this layer still pinned
`v2026.242.1704` — one candy reachable through two refs at two pins, so every resolution reported
`candy … pod-hyprland resolved to multiple versions`. `PickCandyVersion` is newest-wins, so the
**fixed** compositor was always the one composed and behaviour was never wrong; only the pins
disagreed.

Moving one pin at a time is what created the skew in the first place, so both move here.
